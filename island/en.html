A note before we begin

Borrow an island,
come home a new you.

We're writing a book called Play and Prosper: Travel the World, Become Yourself. Somewhere along the way I started thinking: I've spent years traveling the world and becoming myself on my own. Why not do it together with more people, playing and prospering side by side?

I first came to Palawan in 2024, to Puerto Princesa, and fell completely in love. I said right then that I'd come back one day with my family. So many people around me have dreams. Wouldn't it be wonderful to have a small island to ourselves, gather together, and each write our own story?

On September 24 this year, I took a boat to Floral Island and walked from the pier to the dining room, then into every single room. The whole way I kept thinking: imagine having all of this to ourselves and spending a few days here with friends!

Among our friends, some have dreamed of going abroad this year, and some have made wishes of their own. Sea Turtle Guardian Program

When you come to the island,
a nest of baby turtles makes it to the sea.

In El Nido, not far from Floral Island, Sylvia has protected sea turtles on Nacpan Beach for ten years and released more than 70,000 hatchlings. This time, every founding island owner will join her in protecting the turtles.

  1. Every 4 island owners adopt one nest of turtle eggsEach place includes ¥298 donated to the sea turtle conservation center Sylvia founded, and every 4 island owners together adopt a whole nest (50–100 eggs). When the hatchlings emerge and reach the sea, Sylvia records it, and the photos go onto each person's island page, along with an adoption certificate from the center.
  2. Using what we know to help a little moreOne AI workshop group will make a Chinese-language introduction and a short film for the turtle center, so more Chinese speakers get to know these turtles.
  3. A Sea Turtle Guardian certificateOn closing night, everyone receives a "Sea Turtle Guardian" certificate printed with their island number, with a copy on their island page.

In the wild, only about one in a thousand baby turtles grows to adulthood. Friends who stay on in El Nido after 12/11 can visit the turtle center and see its new turtle classroom, opening in early December. Anything beyond the adoptions goes in as a donation, with the details shared on closing night.

Who it's for

This island is waiting for someone like you.

Our founding island owners include Play and Prosper authors, their families and friends, and… you, if something stirred in you as you read this far.

It's for you if…

  • You're at a turning point: you want a different way of living, or want the things you love to support you.
  • You're tired of treating "play" and "earning" as two separate things and want to see if they can come together.
  • You're willing to put your phone down, sit in a circle with people you've just met, and say something true.
  • You're curious about AI, even if you barely use it yet.
  • You love nature and don't mind sandy paths, stairs, or the occasional floor mattress.

It may not be for you if…

  • You're looking for a comfortable resort hotel. This is an island, not a hotel.
  • You want someone to plan everything for you and guarantee how much you'll earn afterward.
  • You just want to sit and listen. For these 5 days, everyone shoots, makes and speaks for themselves.
  • Your health or emotions mean you need to be able to leave at any moment. The island is remote, and there's no leaving midway.

See yourself in the "It's for you" column? You're welcome to apply — we'll have a call and take our time getting to know each other.

FAQ

Questions you might have

How do I get to the island?

If you're flying from China, you'll enter the Philippines through Manila (visa-free entry for Chinese passports currently applies only via Manila and Cebu — please check the latest policy before you travel), and we'll take a chartered bus to Clark. If you're flying from North America or elsewhere, fly into Clark. On December 5 everyone meets and stays one night in Clark, and on December 6 we fly to El Nido together. Flights are booked for everyone through our partner travel agency.

What are the rooms like?

Everyone stays on the island. Rooms have air conditioning, with beds and thick floor mattresses. Couples and close friends can room together; room assignments are shared before opening night.

What will we eat?

Three meals a day for all 5 days are included, healthy food made by the island kitchen, with vegetarian options. If you're vegetarian or have other dietary needs, please note them in your application and we'll let the island know in advance.

Is there internet on the island?

Yes, Starlink satellite internet. We tested it on our scouting trip: it's fine for everyday use and video calls, though it's not 5G. Any accounts and tools you need for the AI workshop will be set up for you before the trip.

What if someone gets sick or injured?

There's a first-aid kit on the island, and two staff members are trained in Red Cross first aid. There's no clinic on the island; the nearest hospital is in Taytay, about 40 minutes at the fastest by boat and car, usually around an hour.

Is there an age requirement?

This season is for adults 18 and over. The boat ride to and from the island is only about 10 minutes, the island has sandy paths and stairs, and the nearest hospital is about an hour away. On the call after you apply, we'll talk through your health together.

How do I choose my island number?

Pick any three-digit number from 001 to 999, first come, first served. Choose one that means something to you: a birthday, a lucky number, a date that matters to your family. On opening night, everyone shares why they chose their number. It stays yours every time you come back to the island.

Can I come if I don't know how to use AI?

Yes. The online prep sessions before the trip will help you set up your accounts and learn the basics, and every group on the island has an AI assistant working alongside you.

What's the refund policy?

¥1,000 deposit: fully refunded if your application isn't accepted. After paying in full, refund requests made before November 15 are refunded in full; after November 15 there are no refunds, because the island's costs are also non-refundable after that date. You can transfer your place to a friend who has gone through the application.
